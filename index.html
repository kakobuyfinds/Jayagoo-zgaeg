Search intent, explained

What are you looking for in a Jayagoo spreadsheet?

A useful shopping spreadsheet brings product links and decision-making details into one place: category, variant, measurements, listed price, source, and the date you checked it.

For readers arriving through a Jayagoo spreadsheet search, the immediate need may be sneakers, a particular clothing category, or accessories. Start with that need rather than opening every listing. The category directory above gives you ten direct routes into CAFANS.

A saved entry should distinguish what the listing says from what you have confirmed. Record unknown details as unknown, and revisit the original listing before placing an order.

Resolve the unknowns

When the picture and the description leave a gap.

If the image looks right but sizing is missing: keep the item in research status. A familiar size label does not establish the measurements of that particular garment.

If the price looks attractive but the total is unclear: record the product price separately. Add only the shipping, service fees, and applicable taxes you can establish for your destination.

If branding is visible but provenance is unclear: do not treat a logo, illustration, or product photograph as authentication. Look for reliable seller information and supporting documentation appropriate to the purchase.

If the saved entry is old: reopen its source. Variants, prices, availability, and policies may have changed since your notes were written.
